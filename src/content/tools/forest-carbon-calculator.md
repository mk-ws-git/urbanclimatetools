## Overview

Developed by The Nature Conservancy, the Forest Carbon Calculator helps estimate the carbon sequestration and storage potential of forest and reforestation projects to support nature-based climate planning.

## Key features

- Carbon sequestration and storage estimates
- Forest-type and management scenarios
- Support for reforestation and conservation planning

## Who it's for

Land managers, NGOs, and cities evaluating urban and peri-urban forest carbon projects.
