## Overview

Climate Just, developed by the University of Leeds, maps social vulnerability to climate impacts at neighbourhood scale so that adaptation resources reach the people most exposed and least able to cope.

## Key features

- Neighbourhood-scale social-vulnerability mapping
- Flood and heat "disadvantage" indices
- Guidance on socially just adaptation
- UK-wide coverage

## Who it's for

Local authorities and community organisations embedding equity in adaptation planning.

Browse the maps and guidance at [climatejust.org.uk](https://www.climatejust.org.uk).
