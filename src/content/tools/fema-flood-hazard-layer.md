## Overview

The National Flood Hazard Layer (NFHL) is a geospatial database of current effective flood hazard data, provided by FEMA to support the National Flood Insurance Program. FEMA says it covers over 90% of the U.S. population.

## Key features

- Search an address on the FEMA Map Service Center to see its flood hazard data or download a FIRMette, a smaller printable version of a Flood Insurance Rate Map
- An NFHL viewer to view, download and print data
- County and state data downloads in shapefile format for GIS use
- GIS web services and a KMZ file for Google Earth

## Who it's for

Property owners, community officials and GIS users in the United States. Official flood zones can miss areas that flood in practice: an NRDC analysis found that nearly 65% of the area flooded by Hurricane Sandy in New York City lay outside the 100-year zone.

## Getting started

Start from FEMA's [NFHL page](https://www.fema.gov/flood-maps/national-flood-hazard-layer), which links to the Map Service Center and data downloads.
