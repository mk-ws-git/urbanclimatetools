## Overview

The Roof Savings Calculator (RSC) is a free web tool developed for the U.S. Department of Energy by Oak Ridge National Laboratory with Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory and White Box Technologies. It simulates a building's roof and attic to estimate the heating and cooling energy savings of switching to a cool (high-reflectance) roof.

## Key features

- Whole-building roof and attic simulation built on the DOE-2.1E engine with AtticSim
- Savings by location and climate
- Compares roof types, reflectance and insulation options
- Free to use in the browser

## Who it's for

Building owners, roofing professionals and sustainability officers evaluating cool-roof programmes. Savings apply only to buildings with heating and/or cooling.

## Getting started

See the [Oak Ridge National Laboratory page](https://www.ornl.gov/content/roof-savings-calculator) for the calculator. For wider background on cool surfaces, see the [LBNL Heat Island Group](https://heatisland.lbl.gov/coolscience/cool-roofs).
