## Overview

ENVI-met is a three-dimensional microclimate model that simulates the interactions between buildings, vegetation, surfaces, and the atmosphere at metre-scale resolution. It is widely used to test how design choices — tree planting, cool materials, building massing, water features — change local temperatures and pedestrian thermal comfort.

## Key features

- Coupled fluid dynamics, thermodynamics, plant physiology, and soil science in one model
- Thermal-comfort indices (PET, UTCI) for heat-adaptation studies
- A free tier (ENVI-met LITE) alongside paid research and professional licenses

## Who it's for

Researchers, landscape architects, and urban designers evaluating heat-mitigation interventions before construction.

## Getting started

Download the software from [envi-met.com](https://envi-met.com); the technical knowledge base at envi-met.info documents model setup and example projects.
