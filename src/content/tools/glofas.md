## Overview

GloFAS is part of the Copernicus Emergency Management Service. It produces global river discharge forecasts, and its data are available under a free and open licence through the Copernicus Climate Data Store.

## Key features

- Real-time forecasts with lead times of 1 to 30 days on a 0.1° (about 10 km) grid
- Reforecast datasets for evaluating skill and setting thresholds
- A separate seasonal product with outlooks up to 16 weeks ahead
- Published evaluations report skill in over 93% of catchments at 1 to 15 days, falling at longer lead times and varying by region

## Who it's for

Flood forecasters, humanitarian agencies and city teams needing early signals for large river floods, especially where local gauges are sparse. It is coarse for neighbourhood-scale urban flooding.

## Getting started

Open the [GloFAS portal](https://global-flood.emergency.copernicus.eu/) for forecasts, or download data from the [Climate Data Store](https://ewds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/cems-glofas-reforecast).
