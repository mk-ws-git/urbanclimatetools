## Overview

The UN Environment Programme's urban resilience resources offer a global knowledge base of tools, case studies, and financing guidance to help cities — particularly in the Global South — strengthen climate resilience.

## Key features

- Global guidance and toolkits
- City case studies across regions
- Climate-finance and investment guidance
- Equity and Global South focus

## Who it's for

City governments and practitioners seeking internationally vetted resilience guidance.
