## Overview

Tree Equity Score, created by the nonprofit American Forests, is a 0 to 100 measure of how well urban tree canopy benefits reach the communities that need them most. Lower scores indicate greater need for tree investment.

## Key features

- Combines tree canopy, building density, income and employment, race, surface temperature, health, language and age data
- National Explorer covering urban neighbourhoods across the United States, plus Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands, with a UK version
- Local Analyzers for specific cities and regions to set custom targets, create property-level planting and protection plans and track progress

## Who it's for

Urban foresters, planners and advocates directing planting to the neighbourhoods most exposed to heat and least covered by trees.

## Getting started

Look up scores in the National Explorer at [treeequityscore.org](https://www.treeequityscore.org/).
