## Overview

Aqueduct, from the World Resources Institute, is a suite of water-risk tools built on a peer-reviewed methodology and best-available hydrological data. The Water Risk Atlas maps indicators such as baseline water stress, seasonal variability, riverine and coastal flood risk, and drought across the globe at high resolution.

## Key features

- Aqueduct 4.0 (2023) with updated hydrological inputs and future projections for 2030, 2050, and 2080
- Companion tools: Aqueduct Floods, Aqueduct Food, and Country Rankings
- Customizable maps and downloadable data under an open license

## Who it's for

City water managers, planners, investors, and researchers assessing exposure of places and assets to water-related risk.

## Getting started

Explore the atlas at [wri.org/aqueduct](https://www.wri.org/aqueduct) — pan to your area of interest and toggle indicators, or download the underlying data.
