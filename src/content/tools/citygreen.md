## Overview

CityGreen, from Singapore's Centre for Urban Greenery and Ecology, is a GIS-based tool that quantifies the ecosystem services delivered by urban trees and green spaces — with particular strength in tropical-city cooling and stormwater benefits.

## Key features

- GIS-based mapping of green-space benefits
- Urban-cooling estimates suited to tropical climates
- Stormwater interception modelling
- Ecosystem-service valuation

## Who it's for

Greenery and parks agencies, especially in tropical and subtropical cities.
