## Overview

FloodMap.net is a free global tool that visualises low-elevation areas at risk of flooding under different water-level scenarios, using digital elevation data — useful for quick, worldwide first-pass screening.

## Key features

- Global coverage for any location
- Elevation-based inundation visualisation
- Adjustable water-level scenarios
- No account required

## Who it's for

Anyone needing a fast, global first look at flood exposure before detailed modelling.

## Getting started

Search any location at [floodmap.net](https://www.floodmap.net).
