## Overview

Digital Coast is a NOAA Office for Coastal Management website that brings together coastal data, tools and training for communities addressing coastal issues. It is authorised under the Digital Coast Act.

## Key features

- **Sea Level Calculator:** assesses sea level and flooding now and in the future, and shows significant historical floods
- **Lidar and elevation data:** links to NOAA's data viewer
- **Historical hurricane tracks:** a tool for viewing past storm paths
- **High-resolution land cover:** 1-metre land cover for parts of several U.S. regions
- **Risk communication training:** material on communicating coastal risk

## Who it's for

Coastal planners, emergency managers and GIS analysts, mainly in the United States.

## Getting started

Open [Digital Coast](https://coast.noaa.gov/digitalcoast/) and browse by tool, data or training topic.
