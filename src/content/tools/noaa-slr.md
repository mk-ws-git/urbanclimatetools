## Overview

The NOAA Sea Level Rise Viewer is a free, browser-based mapping tool that lets anyone visualise how rising seas and coastal flooding could affect U.S. shorelines under a range of scenarios — no GIS skills required.

## Key features

- Interactive sea level rise maps from 0 to 10 feet
- Coastal flooding and "high-tide flooding" frequency layers
- Marsh-migration and wetland-impact projections
- Social and economic vulnerability overlays

## Who it's for

Coastal planners, emergency managers, and community groups assessing exposure and communicating risk to the public.

## Getting started

Open the [viewer](https://coast.noaa.gov/slr/), search for a location, and use the scenario slider to explore inundation depth and extent.
