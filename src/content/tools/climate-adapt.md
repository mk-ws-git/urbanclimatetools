## Overview

Climate-ADAPT is the European Climate Adaptation Platform, a partnership between the European Commission and the European Environment Agency (EEA). It brings together data on climate projections, vulnerability, national and transnational adaptation strategies, case studies, and decision-support tools in one place.

## Key features

- Searchable database of adaptation case studies and options
- Access to EU climate-change projections and impact indicators
- Sector- and country-level guidance for adaptation planning

## Who it's for

Governmental decision-makers and the organisations supporting adaptation planning across Europe.

## Getting started

Browse by theme, sector, or country at [climate-adapt.eea.europa.eu](https://climate-adapt.eea.europa.eu).
