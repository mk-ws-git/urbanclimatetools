## Overview

Climate Central is an independent organisation of scientists and journalists that produces accessible climate risk tools and visualisations — including the widely used Coastal Risk Finder and Surging Seas mapping.

## Key features

- Interactive local climate-risk visualisations
- Coastal flood and sea-level mapping (Surging Seas / Risk Finder)
- Heat, precipitation, and extreme-event analytics
- Shareable graphics for public communication

## Who it's for

Communicators, local officials, and the public who need clear, credible risk visuals.

## Getting started

Explore the tools directly at [climatecentral.org](https://www.climatecentral.org).
