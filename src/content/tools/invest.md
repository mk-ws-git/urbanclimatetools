## Overview

InVEST (Integrated Valuation of Ecosystem Services and Trade-offs), from the Natural Capital Project at Stanford, is a suite of free, open-source models that map and value the benefits nature provides. Its Urban Cooling and Urban Flood Risk Mitigation models are used directly in city green-infrastructure planning.

## Key features

- Dedicated urban models for heat mitigation and stormwater retention
- Terrestrial, freshwater, marine, and coastal service models
- Available as a desktop Workbench or the `natcap.invest` Python package

## Who it's for

Analysts and researchers quantifying the value of parks, trees, and green infrastructure to justify investment.

## Getting started

Download the InVEST Workbench from the [Natural Capital Project](https://naturalcapitalalliance.stanford.edu) or install the Python package from the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/natcap/invest).
