## Overview

The Copernicus Climate Change Service is the European Union's official climate intelligence service, operated by ECMWF. It provides free, open access to high-quality climate data, projections, and indicators cities can use to understand current and future risk.

## Key features

- ERA5 global reanalysis: hourly climate variables from 1940 to present (~31 km)
- CMIP6-based multi-model projections with RCP/SSP scenarios to 2100
- Pre-computed urban indices (cooling degree days, heat-wave frequency, extreme precipitation)
- Climate Data Store (CDS) API and in-browser toolbox

## Who it's for

Climate scientists, municipal planners, and researchers needing reliable baseline and future climate data.

## Getting started

Register for free at the [Climate Data Store](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu) to access datasets and the Python/R client; the CDS Toolbox processes data in-browser.
