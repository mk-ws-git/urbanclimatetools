## Overview

The City Energy Project (an initiative of NRDC and partners) provides dashboards and resources to help cities track, benchmark, and reduce energy use and carbon emissions from their building stock.

## Key features

- Municipal energy benchmarking
- Emissions tracking and reporting
- Progress dashboards
- Policy and program resources

## Who it's for

City energy and sustainability offices managing building-sector decarbonisation.
