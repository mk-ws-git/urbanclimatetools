## Overview

The Georgetown Climate Center's Green Infrastructure Toolkit collects best practices from U.S. cities for planning and scaling green infrastructure such as rain gardens, green roofs and bioswales, with a focus on the policy and legal tools local governments can use.

## Key features

- Case examples from cities across the U.S.
- A "Getting Started" guide to pilot projects
- A "Scaling Up" section on legal and policy tools
- Free to read online

## Who it's for

Local government staff and planners designing programmes and ordinances for green infrastructure.
