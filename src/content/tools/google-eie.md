## Overview

Environmental Insights Explorer (EIE) is a free tool from Google that helps cities and regions measure emissions sources and identify reduction strategies using Google's exclusive datasets and modelling. It estimates building and transport emissions, rooftop solar potential, and tree canopy for thousands of places.

## Key features

- Building emissions modelled from heating, cooling, and powering residential and non-residential structures
- Transportation emissions estimated for trips starting or ending in the city
- Rooftop solar and tree-canopy analyses to prioritise interventions

## Who it's for

Urban planners, sustainability officers, and local-government decision-makers leading city climate action.

## Getting started

Search for your city at [insights.sustainability.google](https://insights.sustainability.google) to view available data.
