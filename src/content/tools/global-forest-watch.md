## Overview

Global Forest Watch (GFW), convened by the World Resources Institute, is a free online platform that combines satellite data, open data, and crowdsourcing to monitor forests in near-real time. Cities and regions use it to track urban tree canopy, forest loss, and the carbon implications of land-cover change.

## Key features

- Annual tree-cover-loss and -gain layers derived from Landsat and other satellites
- Deforestation and fire alerts (GLAD, VIIRS) for custom areas
- Dashboards with carbon-flux and canopy statistics you can export

## Who it's for

Urban foresters, environmental departments, and researchers monitoring green cover and land-use change over time.

## Getting started

Open [globalforestwatch.org](https://www.globalforestwatch.org), draw or upload your area of interest, and open its dashboard.
