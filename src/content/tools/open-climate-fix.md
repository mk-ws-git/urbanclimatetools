## Overview

Open Climate Fix is a non-profit building open-source machine-learning models that nowcast solar photovoltaic output. Better short-term forecasts let grid operators rely less on fossil-fuel standby generation.

## Key features

- Satellite- and weather-driven solar nowcasting
- Fully open-source models and datasets
- Focus on reducing grid carbon intensity
- Active research and contributor community

## Who it's for

Energy researchers, grid operators, and ML practitioners working on decarbonisation.

Browse the code and models on [GitHub](https://github.com/openclimatefix) or read more at [openclimatefix.org](https://openclimatefix.org).
