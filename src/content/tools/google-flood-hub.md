## Overview

Flood Hub is Google Research's public flood-forecasting service. AI models trained on public weather products, river-gauge records and satellite imagery predict river floods up to seven days ahead, updated daily. Crucially, the models transfer skill from data-rich to data-scarce basins, so coverage reaches many places that have no gauge network of their own — it currently spans river basins in more than 150 countries, reaching a population of around 700 million.

## What it does

- **Forecast:** search any covered location for current and up-to-7-day river-flood risk.
- **Visualise:** inundation maps (extent and depth) where the underlying data supports them.
- **Alert:** feeds warnings used by governments, aid organisations and at-risk residents.

## Who it's for

Anyone needing flood lead time where official gauged forecasting is thin — especially planners and responders in lower- and middle-income countries. It is a **supplement**, not a replacement for national civil-protection alerts.

## Getting started

Open Flood Hub, search your location, and read the current forecast — no account needed, no cost. For model detail see the linked Flood Forecasting research page. Complements the US-specific FEMA and GloFAS entries in this catalogue with genuinely global coverage.
