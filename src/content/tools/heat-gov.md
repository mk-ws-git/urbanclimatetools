## Overview

Heat.gov is the website of the National Integrated Heat Health Information System (NIHHIS), a federal interagency effort including NOAA. It publishes heat information and the results of community-led urban heat island mapping campaigns.

## Key features

- Open-access data and reports from urban heat island mapping campaigns
- NOAA, through NIHHIS, has funded CAPA Strategies to support more than 70 community-led campaigns since 2017
- Campaigns run in summer, with local lead organisations such as universities, nonprofits or city governments; CAPA supplies equipment, training and data processing
- Cities have used results for tree planting, siting cooling shelters and heat action plans

## Who it's for

City sustainability and public health staff, researchers and community groups. Check Heat.gov for the current campaign schedule and application rules.
