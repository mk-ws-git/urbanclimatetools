## Overview

The Green Infrastructure Coalition provides practical tools, guidance, and policy resources to help communities plan and implement green infrastructure — from bioswales and rain gardens to urban tree canopy.

## Key features

- Implementation guidance and design resources
- Policy and funding templates
- Community-scale planning support

## Who it's for

Municipal staff and community advocates starting or scaling green-infrastructure programs.
