## Overview

The U.S. Climate Resilience Toolkit is a federal resource that organises climate adaptation around a clear "Steps to Resilience" process, linking practitioners to data, tools, case studies, and funding.

## Key features

- "Steps to Resilience" planning framework
- Curated directory of climate tools and data
- Real-world case studies
- Pointers to funding and expert help

## Who it's for

Planners and community leaders who want a structured starting point for adaptation work.

## Getting started

Begin with the framework at [toolkit.climate.gov](https://toolkit.climate.gov).
