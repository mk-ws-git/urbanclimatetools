## Overview

The Cool Roofs Calculator from Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory estimates the energy, cost, and urban-heat benefits of replacing conventional roofing with high-reflectance ("cool") materials.

## Key features

- Energy and cost-savings estimates by climate zone
- Surface-temperature and cooling-load reduction
- Carbon-reduction calculations
- Backed by LBNL Heat Island Group research

## Who it's for

Building owners, sustainability officers, and policymakers evaluating cool-roof programs.

## Getting started

Run the calculator at [coolroofs.lbl.gov](https://coolroofs.lbl.gov).
