## Overview

The European Environment Agency's urban heat island resources let cities map and analyse heat-island intensity at city and neighbourhood scale using satellite thermal data, supporting targeted cooling interventions.

## Key features

- Satellite land-surface-temperature mapping
- Heat-island intensity and hotspot identification
- Neighbourhood-level comparisons
- Links to urban-adaptation guidance

## Who it's for

City climate and planning teams prioritising where to deploy shade, cool surfaces, and greening.

## Getting started

Access the data and guidance via the [EEA urban environment portal](https://www.eea.europa.eu/themes/sustainability-transitions/urban-environment).
