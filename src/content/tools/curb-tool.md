## Overview

CURB (Climate Action for Urban Sustainability) is a free, interactive planning tool developed by the World Bank with AECOM, Bloomberg Philanthropies, and the C40 Cities Climate Leadership Group. It lets a city map out different climate action plans and compare their cost, feasibility, and emissions impact before committing.

## Key features

- Integrated planning across six sectors: private buildings, municipal buildings and public lighting, electricity generation, solid waste, transportation, and water/wastewater
- Works with a city's own baseline data or built-in defaults
- Used or being adopted by more than 100 cities worldwide

## Who it's for

City sustainability officers and planners building or costing a climate action plan.
