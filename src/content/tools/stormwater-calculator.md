## Overview

The EPA National Stormwater Calculator estimates the annual amount of rainwater and frequency of runoff from a specific site, and shows how low-impact development (LID) controls can reduce it. It draws on national soil, topography, and rainfall datasets.

## Key features

- Site-specific runoff calculations from national datasets
- Modelling of green-infrastructure / LID controls (rain gardens, permeable pavement, cisterns)
- Flood-risk and water-quality improvement analysis
- Built-in climate-change scenario comparisons

## Who it's for

Site designers, municipal stormwater staff, and developers evaluating green-infrastructure options.

## Getting started

Use the [web version](https://www.epa.gov/water-research/national-stormwater-calculator) or download the desktop app, enter a location, and add LID controls to compare scenarios.
