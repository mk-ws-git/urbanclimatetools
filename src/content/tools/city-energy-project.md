## Overview

The City Energy Project was a national initiative led by the Natural Resources Defense Council (NRDC) and the Institute for Market Transformation (IMT) to help cities improve energy efficiency in large buildings. Its first phase worked with ten U.S. cities, including Atlanta, Boston, Chicago, Denver, Houston, Kansas City, Los Angeles, Orlando, Philadelphia and Salt Lake City.

## Key features

- Benchmarking and transparency resource library: how-to guides, templates and city examples
- City-specific policy and programme design support
- Evidence that benchmarking programmes reduce utility costs

## Who it's for

City sustainability and energy staff designing building benchmarking and disclosure policies.
