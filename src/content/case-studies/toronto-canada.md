## Background

In 2009 Toronto became the **first city in North America to legally require green roofs** on new development, aiming to cut stormwater runoff, reduce the urban heat island, and improve building energy performance.

## The intervention

- **Mandatory green roofs** on new commercial, institutional, and residential buildings above 2,000 m² gross floor area
- A **graduated requirement** covering 20–60% of available roof space depending on building size
- A parallel **Eco-Roof Incentive Program** offering grants for green and cool roofs on existing buildings

## Results

The bylaw and incentive program together produced **more than 1,200 green roofs** and around **111,400 m²** of new green roof area — a substantial, measurable expansion of the city's green infrastructure.

## Lessons

Toronto proved a mandate can rapidly scale green roofs. But in 2025 the **Province of Ontario repealed the bylaw**, a reminder that even successful municipal climate policy can be undone by higher levels of government — durable results need broad political and legal foundations.
