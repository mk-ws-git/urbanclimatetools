## Background

On Auckland Anniversary weekend in January 2023, record rainfall caused severe flooding. Single-day totals reported across stations range from about 245 to 258 mm, and January 2023 was the wettest month on record for central Auckland, with 539 mm. Four people died in the floods, and Cyclone Gabrielle weeks later killed 11 more across New Zealand.

## The response

- **Property categorisation:** the council and government classified affected properties, with Category 3 meaning high risk where a house should be removed or not rebuilt
- **Voluntary buyouts:** buyouts are voluntary and cost-shared between the Crown and the council; the Crown agreed to put up to $387 million toward Auckland's Category 3 purchases within a larger recovery package
- **Scale:** early estimates were about 700 homes nationally and around 400 in Auckland; by January 2025 close to 1,000 Auckland homes were eligible for buyout and 123 for grants to make their homes safer, with about 500 still awaiting categorisation
- **First buyouts:** the first four Auckland properties were bought out in December 2023

## Challenges

Buyouts are slow, and some insurers said they would no longer insure Category 3 properties. Counts changed over time as categorisation continued, so check Auckland Council for the current numbers.

## Lessons

Managed retreat from the highest-risk land can be offered voluntarily and funded jointly, but it needs clear risk categories early and faster processing to reduce uncertainty for owners.
