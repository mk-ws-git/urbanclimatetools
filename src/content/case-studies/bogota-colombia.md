## Background

Bogotá sits at around 2,600 metres, so its climate is temperate, but rapid growth, traffic and sharp inequality between localities make green space and air quality an equity issue as much as a climate one.

## The approach

- **Tree target:** the climate action plan aims to plant 670,000 trees over the administration's term; the city reported about 401,000 planted by July 2023
- **Urban forests:** a strategy to consolidate 21 urban forests across about 452 hectares of potential sites, using native species
- **Neighbourhood work:** recent projects in southwestern Bosa use planting as a buffer between motorways and homes

## The equity gap

A Bogotá Chamber of Commerce analysis found that trees are distributed very unevenly: Santa Fe has roughly two trees per resident, while in Bosa about seven residents share a tree. Citywide, there is about half a tree for every three inhabitants.

## Lessons

Planting targets are only a starting point. Tracking who gets the trees, and measuring survival and cooling results, is what shows whether a greening programme actually reduces inequality. Published locality-level temperature results were not found.
